AgriBook is a smart digital solution designed to streamline the agricultural supply chain. It connects farmers directly with procurement centers to eliminate long wait times, manage queues efficiently, and bring transparency to the entire crop selling process.

---

## 🔄 The Traditional Workflow (The Challenge)

Currently, farmers face a long and unpredictable journey when trying to sell their harvest:
`Load Truck` ➡️ `Travel to Procurement Centre` ➡️ `Wait in Queue` ➡️ `Check Acceptance/Rejection` ➡️ `Unload & Weigh` ➡️ `Quality Assessment` ➡️ `Procurement & Payment`

---

## ❌ The Core Problems We Are Solving

AgriBook was built to directly address the major pain points in the traditional system:

- **Long Waiting Times:** Reduces the hours or days farmers spend waiting at procurement centers.
- **Lack of Information:** Provides clear, up-to-date procurement schedules so farmers know exactly when to arrive.
- **No Real-Time Status Updates:** Gives live tracking so farmers aren't left guessing about their spot in line.
- **Inefficient Queue Management:** Stops overcrowding and traffic congestion at the centers.
- **Unclear Payment Tracking:** Fixes the lack of proper, transparent payment status tracking.

---

## ✨ Our Solution (Key Features)

- 📅 **Smart Scheduling:** Farmers can view open dates and plan their trip in advance.
- 🚦 **Live Queue Tracking:** Real-time updates on line lengths and center status.
- 📱 **Instant Notifications:** Immediate alerts for crop acceptance, weight verification, and quality checks.
- 💳 **Digital Payment Tracker:** Transparent, step-by-step logs of payment processing.
